Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

This policy explains what personal data we collect on telemex.health, why we collect it, how long we keep it, and the rights you have. It covers this marketing website only — hospital EMR deployments are governed by separate agreements with each hospital.

1. Who controls your data

Telemex is the controller of personal data collected through this website. Contact us about privacy at telemex.health@gmail.com with the subject line “Privacy request”.

2. Scope — website vs hospital EMR

  • This policy applies to telemex.health: browsing, demo requests, and emails you send us about the product.
  • It does not apply to patient data inside a hospital’s Telemex deployment. Each white-label deployment has its own database, credentials, and configuration, and the hospital governs patient data under its own privacy notice plus a written data-processing agreement with us. If you are a patient, contact your hospital directly about your medical record.
  • Do not send patient health information through the demo form or website contact email. We only need operational context (workflow, teams, governance, current system).

3. Data we collect

a) Demo requests (you provide this):

  • Full name, work email, hospital or health system, your role.
  • Free-text context about your consultation workflow, teams, governance, or current system.
  • Your consent statement and the page URL and timestamp of submission.

b) Correspondence: the content of emails you send to telemex.health@gmail.com and our replies.

c) Technical data (collected automatically in limited form): basic server/hosting logs needed for security (e.g. IP address, user agent, pages requested). We do not run advertising trackers on this site.

All demo-form fields except the free-text context field are required. Without them we cannot respond to your enquiry.

You may withdraw consent for marketing contact at any time by emailing telemex.health@gmail.com. Withdrawing consent does not affect prior lawful processing.

5. Processors and sharing

We share website data only as needed to run this site and respond to you:

  • HubSpot (EU1 shard) — demo-form submissions are sent to api-eu1.hsforms.com (portal 147554530) so enquiries are stored and routed to our team. HubSpot acts as our processor under its Data Processing Agreement and stores data in the EU.
  • Hosting / infrastructure provider — serves the static site and transport-level logs.
  • Google Fonts — delivers fonts when pages load; Google may receive your IP address and user agent for that purpose.

We do not sell personal data collected on this site, and we do not share it with advertisers. We disclose data to authorities only where required by law.

6. Cookies and analytics

  • We do not set advertising or cross-site tracking cookies on this site, and we do not currently run product analytics on the marketing pages.
  • Strictly necessary, transient storage may be used by your browser, our host, or embedded fonts to deliver pages securely. HubSpot may set cookies only if you interact with HubSpot-hosted content; the custom demo form on this page submits server-to-server and does not itself drop marketing cookies.
  • You can block cookies in your browser settings; the site remains readable if you do.

7. Retention

  • Demo enquiries: kept for up to 24 months from last contact so we can follow up, then deleted or de-identified, unless a hospital evaluation or contract requires longer retention (which will be stated in that agreement).
  • Emails: kept for the duration of the conversation plus up to 24 months.
  • Security logs: kept for up to 12 months, then deleted or aggregated.
  • Where law requires longer retention (e.g. tax or dispute records), we keep only what is required.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access, correct, or delete your personal data.
  • Restrict or object to processing, and to data portability.
  • Withdraw consent for contact at any time.
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

To exercise a right, email telemex.health@gmail.com from the address you used, stating what you want. We respond within 30 days and may need to verify your identity. HubSpot-stored enquiries are deleted on request unless we must keep them by law.

9. Security

  • Demo submissions travel over HTTPS to HubSpot’s EU endpoint. Access to enquiries is limited to team members who need it to respond.
  • The site uses a honeypot field and a minimum time-to-submit check to reduce spam; no CAPTCHA content is shown to you.
  • No method of transmission or storage is fully secure. Do not send sensitive, secret, or patient data through website forms.

10. International transfers

Our enquiry processor (HubSpot EU1) stores data in the EU. If our team accesses enquiries from another country, access is limited, over encrypted connections, and protected by the processor’s safeguards and our internal access controls.

11. Contact and changes

Privacy questions or requests: telemex.health@gmail.com (“Privacy request”).

We will post any updated policy on this page and change the “Last updated” date. Material changes affecting pending enquiries will be noted here. Continued use of the site after an update means you have been notified of the revised policy.
