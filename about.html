The path

From a consultation
note to an operating platform.

Each step added a department without replacing the record underneath. That is why packages can be added later without a rebuild.

  1. Foundation

    The clinical record

    Structured consultation documentation, coded diagnosis, orders and the longitudinal chart. That is the part that has to be right before anything else can be.

  2. Hospital

    Outpatient care

    Scheduling, queues, nursing pre-consult and referral flow around the same encounter record.

  3. Laboratory

    Diagnostics

    Requests, specimen and container tracking, named verification and report delivery, and it is the laboratory package available today.

  4. Platform

    Finance and governance

    Service catalogue, invoices, receipts and reconciliation, with role-based access and an append-only audit across every department.

  5. Next

    Pharmacy, inpatient, imaging

    On the roadmap and labelled as such. Hospital Pharmacy is the nearest, with review, dispensing and stock control.
