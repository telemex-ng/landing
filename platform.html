Laboratory

Requests in,
results back out.

Laboratory work is a department workflow, not a mailbox. Requests arrive with the patient attached; specimens, verification and release stay connected to that patient.

  • 01
    Request to worklistAccepted orders appear on the bench without a re-typing step.
  • 02
    Specimen and container trackingCollection, container and processing state recorded as it happens.
  • 03
    Named verificationResults are signed before release; amendments keep their own trail.
  • 04
    Report deliveryThe released report lands in the same record the clinic ordered from.
