Inside the platform

The record moves
with the patient.

Registration fills the chart. The chart fills the order. The order fills the result, the prescription and the invoice. Nothing is copied between them.

Outpatient

The encounter, documented once.

Scheduling, queue, nursing pre-consult and the consultation itself sit in one workspace, with the patient’s history already open.

  • Queue to chart: the patient arrives and the record is already there.
  • Coded decisions: diagnosis, medication and investigation choices recorded as data.
  • Open orders: laboratory, prescription and referral leaving the room attached to the patient.

From request to released result.

  • Specimens: container, collection and processing state.
  • Verification: a named sign-off before anything is released.

Money follows the same record.
