Hospital EMR questions

Before we talk.

Every hospital has different workflows and governance. We’ll map Telemex to yours in a working session.

Ask our team →

The clinical record is created inside the encounter workflow. History, physical examination, investigations, diagnosis, treatment, orders, prescriptions, referrals, and finalisation remain connected to the same consultation and longitudinal patient chart.

Telemex supports coded diagnoses, problem lists, structured allergies, vitals, immunisations, active medications, investigation orders and results, referrals, visit summaries, and a cross-encounter patient timeline.

Prescription workflows can evaluate allergy, duplicate, interaction, age, and pregnancy rules. Blocking warnings require a reasoned override, which is retained in the audit trail.

Selected doctor-side chart modules support offline work. Writes keep their idempotency keys, conflicts use server-wins handling, and clinical decision support is re-validated by the server when the device reconnects.

The current deployment model gives each hospital its own frontend, backend, database, environment settings, credentials, assets, brand configuration, enabled modules, and workflow settings.
